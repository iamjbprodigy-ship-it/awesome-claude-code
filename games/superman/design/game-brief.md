# Game Brief: Superman Over Metropolis

**One-sentence pitch:** You are Superman in a living, fully destructible Metropolis at golden hour, where every power has real physical consequences and the hardest thing you can do is hold back.

## Core loop
- An emergency breaks out somewhere in the city (a fire with people trapped, a falling helicopter, a meteor, an armed robbery). You hear it, fly there and fix it with the right power.
- How cleanly you fix it (people saved, people hurt, dollars of damage, time taken) earns a bronze, silver or gold medal and moves Hope up or down.
- Hope and medals unlock better powers. Clean saves build a streak, and the next emergency starts.
- Between emergencies, the city is a physics sandbox to fly through, explore and (at a cost) wreck.

## Player goal & fail state — what "working" looks like
- Goal: keep Hope high and the streak alive. A session feels complete when the Daily Planet front page (pause screen) reads as a good day for the city.
- Fail: an emergency times out or goes wrong (the fire takes the trapped people, the meteor lands, the robbers escape). Hope drops and the streak resets. There is no game over.

## MVP — what must exist to be the game
- Flight with weight and speed: the sound barrier, sonic boom, wakes, clouds, superhero landing.
- A destructible city with structural collapse (13,000 sleeping plates, about 600 live physics bodies).
- The full power set: strength, grab and throw by weight, heat vision, freeze breath, x-ray, hearing, clap, slow time, invulnerability.
- Four emergency types run one at a time, with a timer and medals.
- One ledger (saves, injuries, damage in dollars, Hope, streak, time) feeding medals, unlocks and the front page.
- A city that reacts: crowds cheer or scatter, cars drive and stop, pedestrians walk.

## Out of scope — not building this
- No multiplayer, no save system, no story campaign or cutscenes.
- No touch or mobile controls (PC only: keyboard, mouse, gamepad).
- No hand-made 3D assets or texture files: all art is generated in code.
- No villains or boss fights in this version.

## Build order
1. Flight camera and feel.
2. Structural destruction plus the core powers.
3. Hope, the damage ledger, one timed rescue, crowd reactions.
4. The Daily Planet front page, medals, Kryptonite, robber counters and unlocks.
5. A visual fidelity pass to the art bible.

---
**Who it's for / what they feel:** PC players who grew up on Spider-Man and Arkham and have waited for a Superman game that makes them feel powerful *and* responsible.

**Art & audio direction:** Golden-hour, sun-drenched Metropolis in the Insomniac Spider-Man style (warm haze, deep skyline, readable street life), with a classic bright red, blue and yellow hero. Synthesized, physical sound.

**Reference game:** Marvel's Spider-Man (Insomniac): the traversal feel, golden-hour skyline and street-level density. Batman: Arkham: the counter-based fight flow, kept only for robbery calls.
